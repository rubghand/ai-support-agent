# How we protect your data

- **Encryption**: data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Card data**: Ledgerly never stores full card numbers. Card details are tokenized by our PCI DSS Level 1
  certified payment processor.
- **Hosting**: data is hosted in AWS data centers in the US (us-east-1). EU customers on the Scale plan can
  choose EU hosting (Frankfurt).
- **Backups**: encrypted backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 30 days.
- **Compliance**: SOC 2 Type II audited annually. The report is available to Scale customers under NDA.

## Employee access

Support staff can only view your account data when you grant temporary access from **Settings → Security →
Support access**. Access expires automatically after 72 hours.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@ledgerly.example. We run a responsible-disclosure program and respond within 2 business days.

Ledgerly support will **never** ask for your password or full card number.
