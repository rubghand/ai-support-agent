# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

## Turn on 2FA

Go to **Profile → Security → Two-factor authentication** and choose an authenticator app (recommended) or SMS.
Save your **10 backup codes** in a safe place; each code works once.

Owners and Admins can require 2FA for everyone on the account under **Settings → Security**.

## Lost your phone?

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Use a backup code**.
2. After signing in, set up 2FA again with your new device.

If you've lost both your device and your backup codes, the account Owner can reset 2FA for other team members
from **Settings → Team**. If you are the Owner, contact support: for security, Owner 2FA resets require identity
verification and take **up to 3 business days**.

## SSO

On the Scale plan, accounts using SAML SSO enforce 2FA through the identity provider instead.
