# Export your data and privacy requests

## Export

Go to **Settings → Account → Export data**. You can export:

- Invoices and credit notes (CSV, or a ZIP of PDFs)
- Clients (CSV)
- Payments and payouts (CSV)

Large exports are prepared in the background and emailed to you as a download link valid for 7 days.

## Privacy requests (GDPR / CCPA)

Ledgerly acts as a **data processor** for your clients' personal data and as a **data controller** for your own
account data.

- To access or delete **your own** personal data, email privacy@ledgerly.example. We respond within 30 days.
- If one of **your clients** asks you to delete their data, you can delete the client from **Clients**. Invoices
  that were sent are kept with personal data removed, because financial records must be retained for tax
  purposes.

## Data Processing Agreement

A DPA is included in our terms of service for all plans. A signed copy is available on request.
