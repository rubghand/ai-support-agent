# Upgrade or downgrade your plan

Only the account **Owner** can change the plan. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**.

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately. You're charged a **prorated amount** for the rest of the current billing
period, and the next renewal is at the new price.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the **end of the current billing period**. You keep your current features until
then. No partial refund is given for the unused part of the period.

If the lower plan has fewer seats than you currently use, you must remove users before the downgrade takes
effect, or the downgrade is cancelled and you stay on the current plan.

## Switching between monthly and annual

- Monthly → annual: takes effect immediately; the unused monthly amount is credited against the annual charge.
- Annual → monthly: takes effect at the end of the annual term.

## Changing plan mid-cycle

Yes, you can change plans at any time. Upgrades are prorated immediately; downgrades wait for the end of the
billing period.
