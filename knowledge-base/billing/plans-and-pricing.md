# Plans and pricing

| Plan    | Monthly price | Annual price (per month) | Invoices per month | Users     |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------ | --------- |
| Free    | $0            | $0                       | 5                  | 1         |
| Starter | $12           | $10                      | 50                 | 3         |
| Growth  | $39           | $32                      | Unlimited          | 10        |
| Scale   | $99           | $82                      | Unlimited          | Unlimited |

Annual billing saves roughly 17% and is charged once per year upfront.

## What each plan adds

- **Starter**: recurring invoices, removes Ledgerly branding, QuickBooks sync.
- **Growth**: late fees, unlimited templates, card fee surcharging, Zapier, API access.
- **Scale**: SSO (SAML), custom payment domain, discounted card fees, audit log, dedicated account manager.

## Paying for Ledgerly

Subscriptions are charged to a card on file. Scale customers on annual billing can pay by invoice.

If your subscription payment fails, we retry for 14 days. After that, the account moves to the Free plan.
Your data is kept, but features above the Free plan are disabled until payment succeeds.

Prices are in USD and exclude sales tax or VAT. See [Taxes and VAT](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/taxes-vat).
