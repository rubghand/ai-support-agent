# Add-ons

Add-ons are purchased separately from your plan under **Settings → Billing → Add-ons**. Each purchase creates an
order (for example, ORD-1042) that you can see in your billing history.

| Add-on           | Price             | Available on      |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Extra seats      | $6 per seat/month | Starter, Growth   |
| SMS reminders    | $9/month          | Starter and above |
| Advanced reports | $15/month         | Growth            |
| Priority support | $29/month         | Starter, Growth   |
| Data migration   | $149 one-time     | All paid plans    |

Scale includes Advanced reports and Priority support at no extra cost.

## Billing

Recurring add-ons are billed with your plan and prorated when added mid-cycle. The Data migration add-on is a
one-time charge.

## Removing an add-on

Remove an add-on at any time; it stays active until the end of the billing period. For refunds on add-ons, see
the [refund policy](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/refund-policy).
