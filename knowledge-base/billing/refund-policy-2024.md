# Refund policy

We want you to be happy with Ledgerly. If you're not satisfied, you can request a **full refund within 14 days**
of any subscription payment, on any plan, monthly or annual. No questions asked.

Add-ons can be refunded within 7 days of purchase.

To request a refund, email billing@ledgerly.example with your account email. Refunds are issued within 10
business days.
