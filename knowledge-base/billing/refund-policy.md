# Refund policy

This policy applies to payments for **your Ledgerly subscription and add-ons**. It replaces all earlier
versions of the policy as of October 1, 2026.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscription payments are **not refundable**, except for billing errors (for example, a duplicate
charge or being charged after you cancelled). Billing errors are refunded in full.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded on a **prorated basis within 30 days** of the charge. After 30 days, annual plans
are not refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next renewal.

## Add-ons

- Recurring add-ons: refundable within **14 days** of purchase if not used.
- Data migration: refundable in full if the migration has not started.

## How refunds are processed

Refund requests are reviewed by our billing team. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method
within **5–10 business days**. To request a refund, contact support with your order number (ORD-xxxx).

Refunds for your own clients' payments are handled with credit notes; see
[Credit notes](https://docs.ledgerly.example/invoices/credit-notes).
