# Cancel your subscription or delete your account

## Cancel a paid plan

The account Owner can cancel under **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Your plan stays active until
the end of the current billing period, then the account moves to the Free plan. Your invoices, clients, and
reports are kept.

Cancelling does not automatically refund the current period. See the
[refund policy](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/refund-policy).

## Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes all invoices, clients, and payment history after a **30-day grace
period**. During the grace period you can restore the account by signing in.

Before deleting:

- Export your data (see [Export your data](https://docs.ledgerly.example/security/gdpr-data-export)).
- Make sure no payouts are pending.
- Cancel any recurring invoice schedules; clients with autopay will no longer be charged.

To delete, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Only the Owner can delete an account.
