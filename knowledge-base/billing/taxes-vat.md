# Taxes and VAT

This page covers two things: taxes on **your invoices** to clients, and taxes on **your Ledgerly
subscription**.

## Taxes on your invoices

Create tax rates under **Settings → Taxes** (for example, "VAT 20%" or "CA sales tax 7.25%"). You can apply up to
two taxes per line item, and mark a tax as compound.

For EU business-to-business invoices, enable **reverse charge** on the client. Ledgerly removes VAT from the
invoice and adds the required reverse-charge note. Both you and the client need a valid VAT number.

Ledgerly does not file tax returns for you. Use the **Tax summary** report to see tax collected per period.

## Taxes on your subscription

Ledgerly charges sales tax or VAT on subscriptions where required. EU businesses can add a valid VAT number under
**Settings → Billing** to be invoiced without VAT under reverse charge. Tax is not refunded retroactively for
past charges if you add a VAT number later.
