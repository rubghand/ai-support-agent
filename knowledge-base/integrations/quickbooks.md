# QuickBooks Online integration

Available on the **Starter plan and above**.

## What syncs

- Clients → QuickBooks customers
- Invoices and credit notes → QuickBooks invoices and credit memos
- Payments → QuickBooks payments, deposited to the account you choose
- Processing fees → recorded as an expense

Sync is **one-way, from Ledgerly to QuickBooks**. Changes made in QuickBooks are not copied back.

## Connect

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks** and click **Connect**.
2. Sign in to QuickBooks and authorize Ledgerly.
3. Map your tax rates and choose the deposit account for payments.

New records sync within about 15 minutes. You can also click **Sync now**.

## Historical data

When you first connect, you can choose to sync invoices from the last 12 months. Older records are not synced.

If records fail to sync, see [Sync errors](https://docs.ledgerly.example/troubleshooting/sync-errors).
