# Payouts

Money from online payments is sent to your bank account automatically.

## Schedule

- **Default**: daily payouts, each containing payments that settled that day.
- You can switch to weekly (every Monday) or monthly (1st of the month) under **Settings → Payments → Payouts**.
- New accounts have a **7-day hold on the first payout** while we complete risk checks.

## Payout currency

Payouts are made in your account's reporting currency. Payments in other currencies are converted at the
time of settlement (1% conversion fee).

## Changing your bank account

Only the account Owner can change the payout bank account. For security, payouts are paused for **48 hours**
after the bank account is changed.

## A payout is missing

Payouts can take 1–3 business days to appear, depending on your bank. If a payout is marked as **Failed**, we
couldn't deposit it, usually because of incorrect bank details. Update your bank account and the failed payout
will be retried automatically.
