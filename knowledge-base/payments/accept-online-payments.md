# Accept online payments

Ledgerly Payments lets your clients pay invoices online. Go to **Settings → Payments** and click **Activate
Ledgerly Payments**.

## Verification

To activate payments we need to verify your business (a legal requirement for payment processors):

- Business details and tax ID
- Owner's legal name, date of birth, and address
- A bank account for payouts

Verification usually takes **1–2 business days**. You can keep sending invoices in the meantime; the pay
button appears once verification completes.

## Availability

Ledgerly Payments is available for businesses in the US, Canada, the UK, the EU, and Australia. In other
countries, connect your own Stripe account instead. See
[Stripe Connect](https://docs.ledgerly.example/integrations/stripe-connect).

## Fees

Processing fees depend on the payment method. See
[Payment methods and fees](https://docs.ledgerly.example/payments/payment-methods).
