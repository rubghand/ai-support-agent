# Invoice templates and branding

Go to **Settings → Templates** to customize how invoices look.

## What you can customize

- Logo, brand color, and font (6 font options)
- Header and footer text, such as bank details or a thank-you note
- Which columns appear (quantity, unit, discount, tax)
- Language of the invoice labels (14 languages)

## Multiple templates

The Free and Starter plans include one template. Growth and Scale include **unlimited templates**, and you can
set a default template per client.

## Removing Ledgerly branding

Invoices on the Free plan show a small "Powered by Ledgerly" footer. It is removed automatically on any paid
plan.

## Custom domains for payment pages

On the Scale plan, payment links can use your own domain (for example, pay.yourcompany.com). Add a CNAME record
pointing to `pages.ledgerly.example` and verify it under **Settings → Domains**.
