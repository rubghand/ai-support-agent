# Recurring invoices

Recurring invoices are available on the **Starter plan and above**.

## Create a recurring schedule

1. Create an invoice as usual.
2. Before sending, toggle **Make recurring**.
3. Choose a frequency: weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
4. Choose an end condition: never, after a number of invoices, or on a date.

Each new invoice is generated at **06:00 in your account time zone** on the scheduled day and sent
automatically.

## Autopay

If the client saves a card or bank account when paying the first invoice, they can opt in to autopay. Future
invoices in the schedule are charged automatically on the issue date. Clients can turn autopay off at any
time from the payment link.

## Editing a schedule

Changes to a recurring schedule apply to future invoices only. Invoices that were already generated are not
changed.

## Pausing

You can pause a schedule from **Invoices → Recurring**. While paused, no invoices are generated, and skipped
periods are not created retroactively when you resume.
