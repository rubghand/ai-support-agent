# Credit notes

A credit note reduces the amount a client owes, or records money returned to them. Use a credit note to correct
a paid invoice, give a partial discount after the fact, or document a refund to your client.

## Create a credit note

1. Open the invoice.
2. Choose **More → Issue credit note**.
3. Select the line items and amounts to credit. The total cannot exceed the invoice total.
4. Choose what happens to the credit:
   - **Refund to client**: refunds the amount to the original payment method (online payments only).
   - **Keep as credit balance**: applied automatically to the client's next invoice.

Credit notes are numbered separately (CN-0001, CN-0002, ...).

## Note

Credit notes are for **your clients**. If you want a refund of your own Ledgerly subscription, see the
[refund policy](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/refund-policy).
