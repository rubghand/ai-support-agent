# Payment reminders and late fees

## Automatic reminders

Turn on reminders under **Settings → Reminders**. The default schedule is:

- 3 days before the due date
- On the due date
- 7 days after the due date
- 14 days after the due date

You can add up to 6 reminders and edit the email text. Reminders stop automatically when the invoice is paid
or voided. Free plan accounts can use the default schedule but cannot edit it.

## Late fees

Late fees are available on the **Growth plan and above**. You can charge:

- a flat fee (for example, $25), or
- a percentage of the outstanding balance (up to 10%), applied once or every 30 days.

Late fees are added as a separate line item when the invoice becomes overdue. Check your local laws before
charging late fees; some jurisdictions cap them.

## Pausing reminders for one client

Open the client, then toggle **Pause reminders**. This is useful when you've agreed a payment plan.
