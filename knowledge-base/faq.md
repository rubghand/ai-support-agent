# Frequently asked questions

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. New accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan with no credit card required.

## Can I get a refund?

It depends on your plan. Monthly payments aren't refundable except for billing errors; annual plans can be
refunded on a prorated basis within 30 days. See the [refund policy](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/refund-policy).

## Can I change my plan later?

Yes, at any time. See [Upgrade or downgrade](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/change-plan).

## Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Registered nonprofits get 30% off any paid plan. Contact support with proof of nonprofit status.

## Can my clients pay without creating an account?

Yes. Clients pay from a secure link in the invoice email and never need a Ledgerly account.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes, for iOS and Android. You can create and send invoices, record payments, and get notified when invoices are
paid. Reports and settings are only available on the web.

## How do I contact support?

Use the chat in the app. Free plan support is via chat and email with a 2 business day response time. Paid
plans get a response within 1 business day; Priority support (add-on or Scale) responds within 2 hours on
business days.
