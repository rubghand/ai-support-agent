# Send your first invoice

1. Click **New invoice** on the dashboard.
2. Choose a client or create a new one (name and email are required).
3. Add line items with a description, quantity, and unit price. Taxes are applied per line item.
4. Set the payment terms. The default is **Net 30**; you can choose Due on receipt, Net 7, Net 15, Net 30,
   Net 60, or a custom date.
5. Click **Preview** to check the PDF, then **Send**.

Your client receives an email with a secure link to view and pay the invoice. You can see when the invoice is
viewed and paid on the invoice's activity timeline.

## Invoice numbers

Invoices are numbered automatically (INV-0001, INV-0002, ...). You can change the prefix and the next number
under **Settings → Invoices**. Numbers must be unique and cannot be reused, even after an invoice is deleted.
