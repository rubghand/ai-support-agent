# Invite your team

Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite member**. Enter their email and choose a role.

## Roles

| Role       | Can do                                                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Owner      | Everything, including billing, plan changes, and deleting the account    |
| Admin      | Everything except billing and deleting the account                       |
| Accountant | View all invoices and reports, export data; cannot send or edit invoices |
| Member     | Create, edit, and send invoices; cannot see reports or settings          |

Each account has exactly one Owner. To transfer ownership, the current Owner goes to **Settings → Team**, opens
the menu next to an Admin, and selects **Transfer ownership**.

## Seat limits

- Free: 1 user
- Starter: up to 3 users
- Growth: up to 10 users
- Scale: unlimited users

Extra seats on Starter and Growth can be added with the Extra seats add-on. See
[Add-ons](https://docs.ledgerly.example/billing/add-ons).

Invitations expire after 7 days. You can resend an invitation from the Team page.
