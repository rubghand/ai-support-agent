# Create your account

## Sign up

1. Go to app.ledgerly.example/signup.
2. Enter your work email and choose a password (at least 12 characters), or sign up with Google.
3. Confirm your email address using the link we send you. The link expires after 24 hours.

Every new account starts on a **14-day free trial of the Growth plan**. No credit card is required. When the
trial ends, the account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

## Set up your business profile

After confirming your email, add your business details under **Settings → Business profile**:

- Legal business name and address (shown on invoices)
- Tax ID or VAT number (optional, but required for EU reverse-charge invoices)
- Default currency. You can invoice in 40+ currencies, but your **reporting currency cannot be changed** after
  your first invoice is sent.
- Logo (PNG or SVG, max 2 MB)

## Didn't get the confirmation email?

Check your spam folder, then request a new link from the sign-in page. If you still don't receive it, see
[Email delivery problems](https://docs.ledgerly.example/troubleshooting/email-delivery).
