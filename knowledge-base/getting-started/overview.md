# What is Ledgerly?

Ledgerly is an online invoicing and payments platform for freelancers and small businesses. You can create
and send invoices, accept card and bank payments, track who owes you money, and export your books to
accounting software.

## Core features

- **Invoices**: one-off and recurring invoices with custom templates and your own branding.
- **Payments**: customers pay invoices online by card, ACH bank transfer, or SEPA Direct Debit.
- **Reminders**: automatic payment reminders and optional late fees.
- **Reports**: revenue, outstanding balances, and tax summaries.
- **Integrations**: QuickBooks Online, Stripe Connect, Zapier, and a public REST API.

## Who is it for?

Ledgerly is designed for teams of 1 to 50 people. Larger organizations should contact sales about the
Scale plan, which includes SSO and a dedicated account manager.
